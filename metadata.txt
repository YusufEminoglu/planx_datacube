about=DataCube Lab answers the question a single map cannot: where is something getting hotter, cooling down or appearing for the first time? It aggregates events into a fishnet panel or builds an X×Y×T cube from vector or raster time series, then lets you inspect it as a heatmap, a 3D voxel stack or a per-cell series in the Cube Workbench.
    Emerging Hot Spot Analysis sorts every cell into one of 17 pattern classes, local outlier and trend tests show where the change is significant, and ARIMA, random forest or exponential smoothing forecast the next periods. Cubes export to CF-style netCDF with real time coordinates, cell bounds and CRS, ready for further work.
    Manual: https://geophilo.com/planx_datacube/manual.html
